BOUND & BELOVED - Alternative Wedding Special Sat 28th February ▾

Time: 10:00am–5:00pm

Location:Royal Arcade, Boscombe, Bournemouth, BH1 4BT

Our February Witch Market brings together everything you’ve come to know and love - magical makers, skilled practitioners, handcrafted curiosities and a welcoming, inclusive community - with the exciting addition of an Alternative Wedding theme.

Perfect for those planning to jump the broom, tie the knot, handfast, or celebrate love in their own unique way, this special edition of the market will showcase beautifully personalised pieces, one-of-a-kind creations, and thoughtful details to make your ceremony truly yours.

Expect an inspiring array of alternative photographers, customised and matching clothing, weird and wonderful table décor, ritual items, gifts, keepsakes, and magical finishing touches you won’t find on the high street. Whether you’re planning a full ceremony, a vow renewal, or simply love the romance of the unconventional, there will be something to spark your imagination. As always, all are welcome, whether you’re actively planning a celebration or simply coming to soak up the atmosphere, support small businesses, and enjoy a day filled with creativity, magic, and community.

Ostara Witch Market Sun 29th March ▾

Time: 10:00am–5:00pm

Location:Royal Arcade, Boscombe, Bournemouth, BH1 4BT

Ostara marks the return of light and the first stirrings of life beneath the soil. A celebration of renewal, balance, and new beginnings.

This market welcomes spring in all its forms. Blossom and buds sit alongside roots, rain, and the quiet magic of growth unseen. Expect nature-inspired art, botanical creations, seasonal magic, and offerings that honour change, hope, and transformation.
