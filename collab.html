Collaborate with us

Anathema Emporium is built on community, creativity, and connection. Much of what we do grows organically, through conversation, shared ideas, and the wonderful people who find their way to us.

After a recent call-out on our social media, we were truly overwhelmed by the response. Messages, ideas, artwork, products, and offers of collaboration poured in, which was both incredibly heartening and, if we are honest, a little chaotic in the best possible way. To help us give every idea the time and consideration it deserves, we have created this collaboration form.

If you feel aligned with what we do and would like to work with us in some way, we would love to hear from you. Please only fill in the sections that apply to you. Not every collaboration fits every category, and that is completely fine. If you have wholesale price lists or supporting information, these can be uploaded as a document rather than typed into the form.

This form is for you if:

You are an artist or illustrator

If you create artwork, designs, or visual work you feel would sit beautifully within Anathema, whether for products, posters, packaging, or future projects.

You are a maker or supplier

If you have a product you believe we would love to stock in the shop.

This includes handcrafted items, ritual tools, books, curios, or other thoughtfully made goods. Please include your wholesale prices and any relevant ordering information, either within the form or as an uploaded document.

You have a skill or offering

If you have a skill, practice, or service you feel could complement our workshops, events, markets, or wider community. This might include teaching, talks, demonstrations, creative skills, or something a little harder to categorise.

You have an idea you would like to share

Sometimes the best collaborations do not fit neatly into a box. If you have an idea, a proposal, or a spark you feel is worth sharing, this space is for that too.

A few gentle notes

We carefully review every submission, but not every idea will be the right fit for us at the right time, and that is completely okay. Alignment, timing, and practicality all play a part in how we work. We are a small, independent business, and while we may not be able to respond to every submission individually, please know that every form is read with care and appreciation. Thank you for wanting to be part of what we are building. Anathema exists because of community, and this page is one of the ways we invite that conversation to continue.
